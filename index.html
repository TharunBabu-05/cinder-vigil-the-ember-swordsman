Creatures of the night watch
Watchfire shade
A drifting mask drawn to a newly lit fire. One clean cut scatters it.
Reed stalker
Look for the ochre line along the ground. Step aside as it rushes, then catch it on the recovery.
Lantern moth
Its wings flash blue and a dotted line marks its aim. Move across the line as the spark leaves its wings.
Bell warden
Its crown glows before each toll. Dash through the expanding ring and land a crossing lunge before the next toll.
Sound & all controls

Press J or tap Jump while moving to vault the low courtyard rails or cross missing boards. G opens the river atlas. At the survey desk west of the gatehouse court, read Nao’s unfinished chart to start her survey.

Move
WASD, arrows or the touch thumbstick. Push the stick farther from its center to move faster.
Slash / crossing lunge
Click or double-click. Space or the sword button on touch also accepts one or two presses.
Dash
Shift or the double-chevron button on touch
Use a landmark or item
E or the diamond button on touch
Camera
C, Zoom, mouse wheel or + / −
Music & footsteps
M or the sound button. Recorded footsteps change between paving and earth. Blade swishes and the dash share this switch.
Pause / resume
Esc or the pause button
