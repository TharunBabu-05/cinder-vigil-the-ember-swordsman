Field guide

Quest, sound & all controls

Find the three amber diamonds. Kindle each watchfire, drive off the shades, then return to the ferry gate and sound the gong. You start with three vitality.

Move
WASD, arrows or touch pad
Slash / crossing lunge
Click / double-click. Space or the touch Strike button also accepts one or two presses.
Dash
Shift or Dash on touch
Use a fire or gong
E or Use on touch
Camera
C, Zoom, mouse wheel or + / −
Music & footsteps
M or the sound button. Recorded footsteps change between paving and earth. Blade swishes and the dash share this switch.
Pause / resume
Esc or the pause button
